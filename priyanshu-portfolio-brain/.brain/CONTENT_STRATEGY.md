## Editorial rules

Prefer concrete verbs (“built the frontend,” “contributed evidence ingestion”) over vague claims (“innovated,” “revolutionized”). Distinguish team outcomes from individual work in the same sentence or adjacent labels. State project status visibly. Never turn a status into a result. Do not add dates, role durations, collaborator names, employment, or credentials that were not supplied. For unknown information, omit it from public copy rather than showing internal placeholders.

## CTA and microcopy

Use “View live demo” only if the destination is reachable and actually demonstrates the described project; otherwise omit or label “Demo (availability may vary)” after owner review. Repository CTA: “View source on GitHub.” Contact: “Email Priyanshu” and “Call Priyanshu” when phone visibility approved. Avoid dead buttons, “coming soon” for projects unless approved, and generic form success messages without a real form.

## Freshness

Check education status, event naming, project development status, live demos, and external links immediately before launch. Maintain a content owner/source for each claim in the project repository or CMS if later introduced.
